A 100-word monologue for [The Tired Dark Lord](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) — a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections: a throne that pinches his back, a cushion eaten by Gromm, a dawn watched with cold tea instead of a valley conquered, generals demanding a war council while he wants a blanket and silence, invasion paperwork he has to sign twice, knees that hurt just watching the hero stand there.

`main.ts` writes the monologue exactly as written to `/output/entry.txt`.

> You came. Sit. No, not the throne, the stool. The throne pinches my back, and Gromm ate the cushion.
>
> I meant to conquer the valley at dawn. Dawn came. I watched it, cold tea in hand, and thought: the valley can wait. It has waited three hundred years. One more morning won't kill it.
>
> My generals want a war council. I want a blanket. Silence. Someone, anyone, to file the invasion paperwork without asking me to sign it twice.
>
> So. Kneel, hero. Or don't. Kneel slowly. My knees hurt watching you stand there.
>
> The realm falls tomorrow. Tonight, I nap.
